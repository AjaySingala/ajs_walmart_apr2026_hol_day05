Embeddings:
Giving meaning to the tokens given a context.

Blood Bank		- Medicinal
Bank of a River	- water-bodies
ICICI Bank		- Money

Embs are numerical representation of the "location" of the word/token in a gi en multi-dimension vector.

They are like GPS co-ords.
bank: [0.24, 0.56, 0.87]

loan from a bank : bank - [0.24, 0.56, 0.87]
river bank: bank - [0.40, 0.34, 0.98]
blood bank: bank - [0.20, 0.30, 0.50]

Fine tuning:
---
You have to retrain the model on specific data.
Legal firm - have a huge dataset related to legal aspects.
You want a "chatbot" that answers queries related to legal matters ONLY!
	- NOT: 
		- what is the GDP of France?
		- who won the last FIFA WC?
		- What is the latest news?
	- CAN:
		- What is the regulation regarding non-payment of loans?
		
One of top credit rating agencies
	- AAA, AAA-...
	- A very niche way of working.
	- They do not want to share their trade secret.
	- They fine-tuned an existiung LLM with their own dataset (huge)
		- LLama3 - Meta (Facebook)
		- setup on their own environment (cloud)
		- trained it on their data.
		- create their own chatbot (like ChatGPT) that employees use daily.
		- ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot are banned for employees.
		
Why not create your own LLM from scratch?
	- not so easy
	- very expensive (lot of resources, money, people, infra, time)
	- you need to expertise to build the LLM from scratch
	
As part of fine tuning:
	- cleanse the data
	- domain expertise
	- train the LLM on your cleansed data (70%)
	- test it on an unseen dataset (20%)
	- validate on a 3rd unseen dataset (10%)
	- confidence level - answering properly

Fine tuning - defining the behavior of your model
	- default -> You are a Personal Assistant.
	- Enterprise will define a specific role (System Prompt):
		You are an expert Legal Advisor
		Refer to only your training data when answering questions related to legal matters of the company.
		Do not look for answers on the internet and do not make any assumptions.
		
		Provide the answers in a clear and concise manner.
		Keep the tone professional.
