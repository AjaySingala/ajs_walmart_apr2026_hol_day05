Or

To create a new model from scratch is very expensive and time consuming.
- you need a lot of data to train.
- Building the algorithim (NN) for your model. Require expertise.
- resources: people, infra, money.

Take an existing model and retrain on their data - fine-tune.
- they do not start from scratch.
- the model is already built.
- Need some expertise who can clean the data and train (retrain) the model to work with only their data.
